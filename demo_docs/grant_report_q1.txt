QUARTERLY GRANT REPORT - Q1 2025
Water Access Initiative - Rural Karnataka

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This quarter, our water access program reached 12 villages, providing clean water infrastructure to 4,200 beneficiaries. We exceeded our target by 15%.

ACTIVITIES COMPLETED
- Installed 8 borewell hand pumps in Bellary district
- Conducted 24 hygiene awareness workshops
- Trained 18 village water committees on maintenance
- Established 3 new community water testing stations

BENEFICIARY DEMOGRAPHICS
- Total direct beneficiaries: 4,200 (2,150 women, 2,050 men)
- Children under 12: 1,680
- Primary occupation: Agriculture (78%), day labor (18%), other (4%)

CHALLENGES
- Delayed approvals from Panchayat in 2 villages pushed installation to Q2
- Shortage of certified borewell technicians required hiring from neighboring district
- Monsoon forecast suggests need to accelerate Q2 timeline

BUDGET UTILIZATION
Total budget allocated: 18,00,000 INR
Utilized this quarter: 12,50,000 INR (69%)
- Infrastructure: 8,00,000 INR
- Training & workshops: 2,50,000 INR
- Personnel: 1,50,000 INR
- Monitoring & evaluation: 50,000 INR

NEXT QUARTER PRIORITIES
- Complete remaining 4 installations
- Establish water quality baseline in all 12 villages
- Begin Phase 2 site assessments
