EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - SELECTED POLICIES

4.1 VACATION POLICY

Full-time employees accrue vacation time as follows:
- Years 1-3: 10 days per year
- Years 4-7: 15 days per year  
- Years 8+: 20 days per year

Vacation time accrues monthly. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused days to the following year. Unused days beyond this limit are forfeited.

Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance via the HR portal.

4.2 REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligible employees may request remote work arrangements. Approval is at manager discretion based on:
- Job requirements
- Performance history
- Team needs

Remote work agreements are reviewed quarterly. Employees must be available during core business hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time) and maintain a suitable home workspace.

4.3 PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

The company provides $1,500 annually per employee for professional development, including:
- Conference attendance
- Online courses
- Professional certifications
- Industry memberships

Employees must receive manager approval before incurring expenses. Submit receipts within 30 days for reimbursement.
